Support

Questions,
answered straight

Last updated August 15, 2026

We'd rather answer the awkward questions up front than have you find out after checkout. If yours isn't here, email info@kicksondeck.store — a person reads it.

Are these authentic Yeezys?

No, and we will never tell you otherwise. Kicks on Deck sells independently produced footwear built to the same silhouette — what the community calls "reps." We are not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by adidas, Yeezy, Nike, or any trademark holder. If a store sells the same shoe at this price and calls it authentic, that's the store you should worry about, not this one.

What does "1:1" actually mean?

It's the community's shorthand for the top production tier — the build that matches the original last, upper texture, stripe placement and sole shape most closely. It is a description of manufacturing quality, not a claim of authenticity. Our rep vs real 350 V2 breakdown shows exactly which details line up and which ones don't, in photos.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 48 hours. After dispatch, expect 7–14 business days in transit to United States & Canada. You get a tracking number by email the moment the label is created. Full detail on the shipping page.

What size should I order?

Depends on the silhouette, and it genuinely matters — sizing is the single biggest reason a pair comes back. The 350 V2 fits close to true to size; Foam Runners and Slides run big and only come in whole sizes. Use the size guide before you pick, or the deep-dive guides for the 350 V2, Foam Runner and Slides.

Can I return them if they don't fit?

Yes. You have 7 days from delivery to start a return on an unworn pair in its original packaging. Read the returns policy for the exact steps and the handful of things we can't take back.

Is checkout secure?

Card details are entered on Stripe's own hosted checkout page — they never touch this site, and we never see or store a card number. Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link are available if your device supports them.

Do you ship internationally?

Right now we ship to United States & Canada only. If you're outside that, email us — if there's enough demand for your region we'll open it.

Do the shoes come in a box?

They ship in a plain protective box with the shoe, its laces and any accessories the style normally includes. We do not supply branded retail boxes, tags or receipts, and we won't pretend otherwise.

How do I track my order?

Your tracking number arrives by email at dispatch. If it hasn't shown up within 48 hours of ordering, email info@kicksondeck.store with your order number and we'll chase it.

Can I pay with PayPal or Cash App?

Checkout runs through Stripe, which covers cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link and — depending on your region — buy-now-pay-later options. If you need a different method, email us before ordering.

Why are you cheaper than resale?

Because there's no resale market between you and us. Resale prices reflect scarcity and flipper margin, not what a shoe costs to make. We buy from the top production tier, inspect each pair, and price it once.

Is Kicks on Deck legit?

Yes, and here is what that means in practice. We are an independent store based on South Florida's Treasure Coast with one catalogue, one price per pair, a real inbox at info@kicksondeck.store and a phone line at +1 786 577 8035. Checkout runs through Stripe, every order ships tracked within 48 hours, and you get 7 days from delivery to return an unworn pair. We also say the one thing most rep stores will not: every pair here is a replica, labelled as such, and we are not affiliated with adidas or Yeezy.

Where is Kicks on Deck located?

South Florida, on the Treasure Coast. We are an online store and ship to United States & Canada; there is no walk-in showroom. If you are searching "sneaker store near me" for this style of shoe, here is what to actually look for.

Do you sell kids' sizes?

Not yet. The catalogue runs men's US 4 to 13 on the 350 V2 (women's sizes labelled W from W5) and whole sizes on the Foam Runner and Slide. Smaller feet usually work on the 350 V2 in W5 to W7; the Foam Runner sizing guide covers the smallest options there. Vote for kids sizes on the next drop poll and we will stock them when the demand is there.

Foam Runner or Slide, which is more comfortable?

For all-day walking, the Foam Runner: it wraps the whole foot and the vents keep it cool. For pool, beach and quick errands, the Slide: one strap, on and off in a second, and it dries fast. Both run big and come in whole sizes, so size down one. The full comparison is in Foam Runner vs Slides.
