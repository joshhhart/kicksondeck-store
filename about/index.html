The store

About
Kicks on Deck

Last updated August 15, 2026

Kicks on Deck is an independent footwear store run out of South Florida's Treasure Coast. We sell 94 styles across three silhouettes — the 350 V2, the Foam Runner and the Slide — at prices that reflect what a shoe costs to make rather than what a resale market decided it's worth this week.

We say the quiet part out loud

Everything here is a replica. Not "inspired by," not "unauthorised retail," not a wink and a nudge in the product title — a rep, produced independently, sold as such. We are not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by adidas, Yeezy, Nike, or any trademark holder.

That honesty is the whole business model. A store that lies about authenticity has to keep lying — about where stock comes from, about why the box is wrong, about why a refund is complicated. We'd rather tell you exactly what you're buying and let the shoe do the arguing. Our rep vs real breakdown puts our own product under the magnifying glass and shows you where it wins and where it doesn't.

How a pair gets here

Sourced from the top production tier. The rep market runs several quality grades deep. We buy from the highest one — the builds that match the original last, the upper texture, the stripe placement and the sole geometry. It costs more per pair and it's the only part of this we won't compromise on.

Inspected before it ships. Every pair is opened, checked for glue marks, stitching faults, sole seating and colour consistency, then repacked. Pairs that fail don't go out. QC photos on request, before dispatch, on any order.

Dispatched within 48 hours. Free shipping to United States & Canada, tracked, with a 7-day window if the fit isn't right.

What we're not

We're not a marketplace, a dropshipper you've never heard of, or a link in someone's bio that disappears next month. There's one catalogue, one price per pair, a real inbox at info@kicksondeck.store, and policies you can read before you spend anything — shipping, returns, FAQ.

Why the culture stuff

Half this site is a journal — sizing guides, colourway explainers, care instructions, styling. It's there because the questions that stop people buying a $149 pair of sneakers are almost never about the sneakers. They're "will these fit," "how do I keep them clean," "which colourway actually goes with what I own." We wrote all of it so you don't have to ask.

Get in touch

Sizing help, QC photos, order status, a colourway you want us to stock: info@kicksondeck.store, or the contact page. We answer.
