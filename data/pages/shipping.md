## The timeline

| Stage | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Order placed → dispatched | Within {{DISPATCH}} hours |
| In transit | {{TRANSITMIN}}–{{TRANSITMAX}} business days |

Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed on a weekend are dispatched the next business day.

## What it costs

Nothing. Shipping is free to every address in {{COUNTRIES}}, on every order, at every price. Shipping shows as a $0.00 line on your Stripe receipt so the total is never a surprise.

## Where we ship

{{COUNTRIES}}. We do not currently ship anywhere else. If you're outside that, email [{{EMAIL}}]({{EMAILLINK}}) — we open new regions when there's enough demand to do it properly.

## Tracking

You'll get a tracking number by email when the label is created. Tracking can take a day or so to show its first scan — that gap is normal and doesn't mean anything is wrong.

## If a parcel goes quiet

Tracking that hasn't updated in five business days is worth an email. Send your order number to [{{EMAIL}}]({{EMAILLINK}}) and we'll open a trace with the carrier. If a parcel is confirmed lost, we replace it or refund it in full — your choice.

## Wrong address

We ship to the address Stripe collects at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us immediately — before dispatch we can fix it for free, after dispatch we're at the carrier's mercy.

## Duties and customs

Prices are what you pay. There is no separate duty or brokerage invoice waiting for you on delivery within {{COUNTRIES}}.
