## Are these authentic Yeezys?

No, and we will never tell you otherwise. Kicks on Deck sells independently produced footwear built to the same silhouette — what the community calls "reps." We are not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by adidas, Yeezy, Nike, or any trademark holder. If a store sells the same shoe at this price and calls it authentic, that's the store you should worry about, not this one.

## What does "1:1" actually mean?

It's the community's shorthand for the top production tier — the build that matches the original last, upper texture, stripe placement and sole shape most closely. It is a description of manufacturing quality, not a claim of authenticity. Our [rep vs real 350 V2 breakdown](/blog/rep-vs-real-yeezy-350-v2/) shows exactly which details line up and which ones don't, in photos.

## What size should I order?

Depends on the silhouette, and it genuinely matters — sizing is the single biggest reason a pair comes back. The 350 V2 fits close to true to size; Foam Runners and Slides run big and only come in whole sizes. Use the [size guide](/size-guide/) before you pick, or the deep-dive guides for the [350 V2](/blog/yeezy-350-v2-sizing/), [Foam Runner](/blog/yeezy-foam-runner-sizing/) and [Slides](/blog/yeezy-slides-sizing/).

## Is checkout secure?

Card details are entered on Stripe's own hosted checkout page — they never touch this site, and we never see or store a card number. Apple Pay, Google Pay and Link are available if your device supports them.

## Do the shoes come in a box?

They ship in a plain protective box with the shoe, its laces and any accessories the style normally includes. We do not supply branded retail boxes, tags or receipts, and we won't pretend otherwise.

## Can I pay with PayPal or Cash App?

Checkout runs through Stripe, which covers cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link and — depending on your region — buy-now-pay-later options. If you need a different method, email us before ordering.

## Why are you cheaper than resale?

Because there's no resale market between you and us. Resale prices reflect scarcity and flipper margin, not what a shoe costs to make. We buy from the top production tier, inspect each pair, and price it once.
