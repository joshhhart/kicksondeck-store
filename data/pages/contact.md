## What to include

Whatever gets you an answer in one reply instead of four:

- **Order status** — your order number and the email you checked out with.
- **Sizing help** — your usual US size and the brand you're basing it on, plus which silhouette you're looking at. The [size guide](/size-guide/) answers most of these instantly.
- **QC photos** — your order number, before dispatch. Happy to send them on any order.
- **Returns or exchanges** — order number and the size you actually want. Steps are on the [returns page](/returns/).
- **Restock requests** — the colourway and your size. These genuinely decide what we buy next, alongside the [drop vote](/#vote).

## Faster than emailing
