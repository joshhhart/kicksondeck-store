We already did this for the [350 V2](/blog/yeezy-350-v2-reps-what-they-look-like/) — Primeknit and boost are their own thing to judge. The Foam Runner is a completely different build: one piece of molded EVA foam, no laces, no knit. That means the rep "tells" are different too. Here's what actually separates a good 1:1 Foam Runner from a rough one, colorway by colorway.

## The short answer

A strong 1:1 Foam Runner gets three things right: the foam has the naturally **mottled, marbled texture** from injection molding instead of looking like flat, uniform-colored plastic; the **mold seam line** where the two foam halves meet is clean and low, not a rough ridge you can catch a fingernail on; and the sole tread depth matches the retail lug pattern instead of being shallow and smoothed-off. Get those three right and the rest — fit, weight, flex — tends to follow.

## Onyx

[Onyx](/product/onyx-yz-foam-rnnr/) is the one to check foam density on. It's a solid dark colorway with nowhere to hide — an overly soft, spongy foam or a too-rigid one both show up immediately in how the shoe sits and photographs. A good pair holds its shape without looking stiff.

## Ochre

[Ochre](/product/ochre-yz-foam-rnnr/) is a strong color-match test. The retail shade sits in a specific muted mustard-brown range — reps that lean too orange or too yellow are an easy tell from across the room, before you even look at the foam texture up close.

## Ararat

Light neutrals like [Ararat](/product/ararat-yz-foam-rnnr/) show mold seams the fastest — there's no busy color or pattern to distract from a rough parting line around the upper. This is the pair to check if you want to judge mold quality specifically.

![the MX Cream Clay Foam Runner](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0557/4570/8145/products/REP-VERSION-MX-Cream-Clay-Yeezy-Foam-RNNR-Running-Shoes.webp?v=1697957822)

## MX Cream Clay

Multi-tone pairs like [MX Cream Clay](/product/mx-cream-clay-yz-foam-rnnr/) are where factory paint layering gets tested — the mottled effect should look organic and randomized, the way real marbled EVA does, not like a hard-edged, obviously stenciled pattern.

## What "1:1" actually means here

Same answer as always: these are replicas, not retail pairs. "1:1" describes the production tier — factory working from the same pattern and mold references as the highest-grade reps on the market — not a claim that they're the real thing. Our [rep terms glossary](/blog/sneaker-rep-terms-glossary/) breaks down 1:1, OG, UA and QC if any of this is new to you.

## Sizing before you buy

Foam Runners run big and only come in whole sizes, which is a completely separate question from rep quality. Check the [Foam Runner sizing guide](/blog/yeezy-foam-runner-sizing/) before you order — half sizes size down, whole sizes stay true.
