Three colorways have already gone through at least one restock batch, which is your best evidence they can do it again:

- **[Bone (2022/2023 Restock)](/product/bone-2022-2023-restock-yz-slide/)** — the warm neutral, and consistently one of the first Slides to sell through each batch.

## How the next batch gets decided

Same system as the [350 V2 and Foam RNNR restocks](/blog/yeezy-350-v2-restock-guide/): the [vote widget](/#vote) tracks real demand across every silhouette, Slides included. Whatever's climbing gets weighted into the next production run — it's not a raffle or a countdown timer, it's a running poll of what people actually want back.

- **Onyx and Enflame Orange** are the two most-requested Slides not yet on a confirmed restock batch — if you want to see either return, [vote for it](/#vote).
- **Granite and Ochre** show up less often but tend to spike after a styling post — that's usually the earliest signal a colorway is trending toward a restock.

## Before you wait on a restock

A few things worth knowing if you're holding out for a specific colorway instead of buying what's in stock now:

- **Restocked pairs go through the same QC** as first-run stock — a restock is a new production batch, not old inventory pulled from a back room. Same [7-day buyer-protection window](/blog/are-rep-sneakers-worth-it/) applies.
- **Sizing doesn't change between runs.** If you're unsure of your size, read the [Slides sizing notes](/blog/yeezy-slides-sizing/) now so you're not guessing when a restock actually lands.
- **Check the [Slides collection page](/collection/slides/) directly**, not just the homepage — restocked colorways post there first, often before the vote widget updates.

> If a colorway shows as sold out, it's genuinely sold out — voting is the real lever for what comes back, not a countdown gimmick.
