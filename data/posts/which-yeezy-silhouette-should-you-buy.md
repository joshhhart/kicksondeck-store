Three silhouettes, one rotation, and a lot of people asking the same question before they buy their first pair: 350 V2, Foam Runner, or Slides — which one actually fits how you live? We've compared [350 V2 vs Foam Runner](/blog/350-v2-vs-foam-runner/) and [Foam Runner vs Slides](/blog/foam-runner-vs-slides/) separately, but nobody's put all three side by side in one place. Until now.

Short version: the 350 V2 is the sneaker, the Foam Runner is the do-anything slip-on, and the Slide is the shoe you don't think about. Here's how to know which one is your first pair.

![the Beluga 2.0 350 V2](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0557/4570/8145/products/REP-VERSION-Beluga-2_0-Yeezy-Boost-350-V2-Running-Shoes.webp?v=1697960494)

## The 350 V2 — the everyday sneaker

This is the one that started the rotation for most people. Primeknit upper, boost midsole, low profile, laces. It reads as an actual sneaker in a way the other two don't, which means it goes further outside the house — work, travel, dates, anywhere a clog or a slide would look like you gave up. The [Beluga 2.0](/product/beluga-2-0-yz-boost-350-v2/) is one of the most balanced colorways in the lineup, grey-and-orange enough to stand out without trying too hard.

Trade-off: it's a lace-up, so it's not a grab-and-go shoe, and boost needs real care (see the [350 V2 cleaning guide](/blog/how-to-clean-yeezy-350-v2/)) if you don't want it yellowing. Full breakdown of fit in the [350 V2 sizing guide](/blog/yeezy-350-v2-sizing/) and every color option in the [350 V2 colorways guide](/blog/yeezy-350-v2-colorways-guide/).

**Pick the 350 V2 if:** you want one shoe that works for both "leaving the house" and "actual outfit."

## The Foam Runner — the do-anything slip-on

One piece of molded EVA foam, ventilation holes, real tread, and a closed toe. It's the only one of the three you can genuinely run errands in — grocery store, gas station, short walks — without your foot sliding around. The [Ararat](/product/ararat-yz-foam-rnnr/) colorway is a neutral earth tone that pairs with almost anything, which matters since Foam Runners are a statement shoe whether you want them to be or not.

Sizing is the catch: Foam Runners run big for most feet, and the [Foam Runner sizing guide](/blog/yeezy-foam-runner-sizing/) covers exactly how much to size down. Full color lineup is in the [Foam Runner colorways guide](/blog/yeezy-foam-runner-colorways-guide/).

**Pick the Foam Runner if:** you want the most comfortable shoe you own and don't mind it being the loudest thing in the room.

![the Earth Brown Slide](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0557/4570/8145/products/REP-VERSION-Earth-Brown-Yeezy-Slide-Shoes.webp?v=1697958016)

## The Slide — the shoe you don't think about

No laces, no break-in, no decisions. A soft footbed and a single strap. The [Earth Brown](/product/earth-brown-yz-slide/) is the quietest colorway in the lineup — the one you can wear with sweats to the mailbox and not think twice about. Slides aren't trying to be a real outfit; they're the shoe you put on when you've already decided not to try.

They also run big — check the [Yeezy Slides sizing guide](/blog/yeezy-slides-sizing/) before you order — and every colorway is mapped out in the [Slides colorways guide](/blog/yeezy-slides-colorways-guide/).

**Pick Slides if:** you want the lowest-effort shoe in your rotation and you're not walking further than the driveway.

## Which Yeezy silhouette should I buy first?

If you're only buying one pair, get the **350 V2**. It's the most versatile of the three — the only one that convincingly works as an actual outfit shoe, not just a house shoe. Foam Runners and Slides are both excellent second and third pairs once you already have a sneaker in rotation, since neither one covers you for situations that call for laces.

## So which one wins?

There's no universal winner — that's the whole point of having three silhouettes instead of one. Cop the 350 V2 first if you want a real sneaker, add the Foam Runner when you want something you can wear anywhere without laces, and grab Slides once you just want a shoe you never have to think about. Most people who build out the full rotation end up owning all three for exactly that reason.
