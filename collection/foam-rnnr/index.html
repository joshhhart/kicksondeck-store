Before you pick a size

Do Yeezy Foam Runner reps fit true to size?

Foam Runners run big and only come in whole sizes — size down one from your normal US size. If you're between sizes, take the smaller one. The Foam Runner sizing guide on the blog covers women's and half-size conversions.

How much do Foam Runner reps cost at Kicks on Deck?

Every Foam Runner colourway we stock (15 styles) starts at $79, with free shipping to United States & Canada and no resale markup.

Are these authentic Foam Runners?

No. This is an independently produced 1:1 replica and we say so on every page — Kicks on Deck is not affiliated with, authorized by or endorsed by adidas, Yeezy or any trademark holder. You are buying the silhouette and the build quality, at $79 instead of resale.

How fast do Foam Runner orders ship?

Dispatched within 48 hours with tracking emailed at dispatch, then 7–14 business days in transit. Shipping is free to United States & Canada on every order. You have 7 days from delivery to return an unworn pair or swap it for another size. Email info@kicksondeck.store with your order number and we send the return address the same day.
