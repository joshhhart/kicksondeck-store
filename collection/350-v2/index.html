Before you pick a size

Do Yeezy 350 V2 reps fit true to size?

The 350 V2 fits close to true to size — take your normal US size, and go up a half if you have wide feet. Women's sizes are labelled W. The 350 V2 sizing guide on the blog covers women's and half-size conversions.

How much do 350 V2 reps cost at Kicks on Deck?

Every 350 V2 colourway we stock (62 styles) starts at $149, with free shipping to United States & Canada and no resale markup.

Are these authentic 350 V2s?

No. This is an independently produced 1:1 replica and we say so on every page — Kicks on Deck is not affiliated with, authorized by or endorsed by adidas, Yeezy or any trademark holder. You are buying the silhouette and the build quality, at $149 instead of resale.

How fast do 350 V2 orders ship?

Dispatched within 48 hours with tracking emailed at dispatch, then 7–14 business days in transit. Shipping is free to United States & Canada on every order.
