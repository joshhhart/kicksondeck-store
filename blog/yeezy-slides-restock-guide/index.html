Yeezy Slides Restock Guide (2026) — What's Back, What's Rare

Kicks on Deck · August 12, 2026 · 4 min read

The Slide is the easiest Yeezy silhouette to sell out — one strap, one mold, no half-size cushion to spread demand across. When a colorway goes, it goes fast. Here's what's already restocked, what's realistic to come back, and how the process actually works.

What's already come back

Three colorways have already gone through at least one restock batch, which is your best evidence they can do it again:

  • Bone (2022/2023 Restock) — the warm neutral, and consistently one of the first Slides to sell through each batch.
  • Glow Green (2022/2023 Restock) — the chartreuse-leaning neon, back with an updated foam pour from the earlier run.
  • Pure (Restock) — the off-white neutral, restocked because it's the safest first-Slide pick for most people.

How the next batch gets decided

Same system as the 350 V2 and Foam RNNR restocks: the vote widget tracks real demand across every silhouette, Slides included. Whatever's climbing gets weighted into the next production run — it's not a raffle or a countdown timer, it's a running poll of what people actually want back.

  • Onyx and Enflame Orange are the two most-requested Slides not yet on a confirmed restock batch — if you want to see either return, vote for it.
  • Granite and Ochre show up less often but tend to spike after a styling post — that's usually the earliest signal a colorway is trending toward a restock.

Before you wait on a restock

A few things worth knowing if you're holding out for a specific colorway instead of buying what's in stock now:

  • Restocked pairs go through the same QC as first-run stock — a restock is a new production batch, not old inventory pulled from a back room. Same 7-day buyer-protection window applies.
  • Sizing doesn't change between runs. If you're unsure of your size, read the Slides sizing notes now so you're not guessing when a restock actually lands.
  • Check the Slides collection page directly, not just the homepage — restocked colorways post there first, often before the vote widget updates.
If a colorway shows as sold out, it's genuinely sold out — voting is the real lever for what comes back, not a countdown gimmick.
